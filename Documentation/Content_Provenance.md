# KidsLearn TV content provenance

The first twenty lessons and the first ten quiz questions are transcribed from the user-provided `Part B/App Content.pdf`, including required button labels and friendly feedback copy. The additional question pool is newly drafted to satisfy the requirement for **five distinct category-specific questions**, because the supplied ten-question mixed bank has fewer than five per individual category.

No downloaded third-party logos, cartoon images, audio recordings or videos are bundled. Present visuals are Unicode emoji, SF Symbols, native shapes and gradient cards. Spoken narration uses `AVSpeechSynthesizer` and copies the content sheet's textual learning captions. The built-in sample media used by MediMate is generated locally and original.

This is source-level documentation; real-device pronunciation quality, platform-specific emoji rendering and focus accessibility must be reviewed on tvOS. Note that the broader App Description mentions numbers 1–10, while the detailed *App Content* fixes starter lessons at 1–5. The prototype implements the twenty lessons explicitly enumerated in the detailed document; a 1–10 expansion is an enhancement rather than a falsely claimed implemented feature.
